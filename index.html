Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Can you help me remotely?

+
Yes. Many issues can be resolved via secure remote support and a quick call. If it needs in-person attention, I'll tell you upfront.

What if you can't fix my issue?

+
If I can't meaningfully improve things or give you a clear path forward, I'll tell you honestly — no runaround, no wasted time.

Is IT Buddy good for seniors?

+
Absolutely. I move at your pace, repeat as needed, and can write down simple steps you can keep. Many of my favorite clients are seniors!

Do you work with small businesses?

+
Yes — I help with accounts, access, devices, backups, and security basics, all without the cost of a full-time IT hire.

How fast do you respond?

+
Same-day response on business days. Concierge plan clients get priority response for urgent issues.

Can I cancel my plan anytime?

+
Yes — all plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. I want you to stay because I'm delivering value, not because you're locked in.
