Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

A few things small business owners commonly want to know before getting started.

What size businesses do you work with?

+
IT Buddy is designed primarily for small businesses that need dependable IT help but don't have a full internal IT department. The best fit is typically a team of about 1–15 employees.

What happens during the free IT checkup?

+
I review the basics of your accounts, access, MFA, devices, backups, and security setup, then explain what I would prioritize. There's no obligation to sign up for a monthly plan.

Can you support Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace?

+
Yes. I can help with users, email, permissions, MFA, shared files, account administration, onboarding, offboarding, and day-to-day issues.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

+
No. IT Buddy is built around flexible, month-to-month support rather than locking small businesses into long agreements.

Can you help remotely?

+
Yes. Many business and personal IT issues can be resolved securely by remote support. On-site service is also available locally when needed.

Do you still work with home users?

+
Absolutely. Personal tech support is still available for computers, Wi-Fi, printers, account recovery, device setup, migration, and one-on-one coaching.
